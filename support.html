Help with SoundMind - Your Tone, Reinterpreted.

Need help or want to report a TestFlight issue? Email shoshanadean@gmail.com.

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • The SoundMind app version and build number.
  • Whether the issue happened with recording, import, tone generation, playback, export, onboarding, subscription, or library.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.

Current TestFlight notes

Tone reinterpretation is local-first and works from recorded or imported audio. Automatic transcription is paused in the main runtime while the speech adapter is investigated.

Purchases and restore

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Once TestFlight products are configured, purchase and restore behavior should be tested through Apple sandbox/TestFlight flows.
